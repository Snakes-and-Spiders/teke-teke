# Teke Teke

A top-down stealth horror game you play in the browser. You are Teke Teke, the legless spirit of Japanese urban legend, and you hunt the people responsible for your death.

**Play:** https://snakes-and-spiders.github.io/teke-teke/

## Chapters

1. **The Crossing.** The three classmates who left you on the tracks.
2. **The Bureau.** The engineer who signed off on the failed brakes.
3. **The Last Train.** The driver.

**DLC: The Kuroda List.** The yakuza mock your legend. Break into the Kuroda manor, steal the list of the boss's associates, hunt them one by one, then face the boss himself. Every kill spreads the legend, and as it grows, crowds scatter and police lose their nerve.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Crawl |
| Shift | Scuttle (fast, loud) |
| Space | Strike |
| Q | Sense your prey |
| Esc | Pause |
| D on title | Start the DLC |
| 1–9 on title | Jump to a chapter |

It's a single HTML file with no dependencies. Open `index.html` in any modern browser to play offline.

The characters and story are fictional, inspired by the Japanese urban legend.
